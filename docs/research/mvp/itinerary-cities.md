# Itinerary content — San Francisco and San Diego

Written for the MVP idea bank. Two destinations, each a Friday-to-Sunday weekend with two nights,
early October 2026, travelling from Los Angeles without a car.

**Compiled 15 September 2026.** Every cost line carries a source URL and the words
"checked 15 Sep 2026". Where a figure could not be read off the operator's or the venue's own
page it is marked `unverified` with a note on what was tried. Nothing here is estimated and
presented as checked.

**Audience:** USC exchange student, 19–25, foreign passport, often no car, budget-conscious,
first time in California outside Los Angeles. **A large share are under 21.** Anything centred on
a bar, a tasting room or a club is closed to them, and that is stated on the item rather than
left for the door.

Reuses verified transit and Surfliner data from `docs/research/destinations.md` (compiled
3 September 2026) — the USC U-Pass, Surfliner student discount, and the Mexico re-entry note in
particular.

**Field set per item:** `title`, `eyebrow`, `what`, `cost`, `needsCar`, `timeNeeded`, `catch`,
`photoQuery`. Items are in the order you would actually do them: Friday evening, Saturday,
Sunday morning.
